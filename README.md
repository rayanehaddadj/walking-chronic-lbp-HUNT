# Volume and Intensity of Walking and Risk of Chronic Low Back Pain: The HUNT Study

This repository hosts the analytical scripts used in a research study investigating the association between volume and intensity of walking with risk of chronic low back pain. The code supports data processing, statistical analysis, and visualization, ensuring transparency and reproducibility of the findings. All analyses were conducted using Stata 18.5 (StataCorp, College Station, TX, USA).

**Reference: <br>**
Haddadj R, Nordstoga AL, Nilsen TIL, et al. Volume and Intensity of Walking and Risk of Chronic Low Back Pain. JAMA Netw Open. 2025;8(6):e2515592. Available at: https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2835297
